Our story

The oven comes first

Osteria Luna is a thirty-two-seat room built around one oak-fired oven. Flour, semolina and eggs go on the board at ten each morning. The oven is lit at noon and sits near 400°C by dinner.

The menu changes with the market and draws mostly on Puglia, Piedmont and the Lombard lakes. The wine list is twenty small Italian growers, all by the glass. The restaurant is a demo concept, so no table is real.
